Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in time is from 2:00 PM onwards, and check-out is expected by 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out can be requested upon availability.

Is airport shuttle service available?

Yes, we provide VIP airport pickup and drop-off services for all our registered guests. Please inform our concierge in advance.

Are pets allowed in the hotel?

To maintain absolute cleanliness and comfort for all guests, pets are generally not permitted, except for certified service animals.
